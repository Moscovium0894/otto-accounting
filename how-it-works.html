How it works

Connect once.
It runs every night.

Otto plugs into your EPOS, your bank, and your suppliers in a single 30-minute setup call. Software does the data work overnight. A qualified bookkeeper signs off the month the next morning. You see the result before service.

01 · Connect

→

Your till.

We link Toast, Lightspeed, Square, Tevalis, Zonal, or Epos Now. Yesterday's takings post to your books by 9am — split into food, wet, service charge, and tips.

→

Your bank.

A read-only feed from your business account. Every line is reconciled to its source — supplier invoice, till deposit, payroll, card payout, refund.

→

Your suppliers.

We give your suppliers a forwarding email. PDF invoices arrive, get auto-coded against the last one from the same supplier, and match to the bank line when it clears.

02 · Run overnight

⟳

Sales posted by 9am.

Z-reads pull from the till at midnight. Variances against the bank deposit are flagged for review. Tips and service charge are split correctly per your rules.

⟳

Invoices coded.

Each line item is matched against your chart of accounts. Recurring suppliers code identically every week; the weird ones are held for your bookkeeper.

⟳

Bank reconciled.

Every transaction matched. Open items flagged. Nothing carries to the next morning without an explanation.

03 · Reviewed & signed

✓

Your bookkeeper checks it.

Sarah, James, Priya — whoever's assigned to your kitchen reviews the night's work. Same person every month. Their name is on the report.

✓

Held items get a question.

If something needs your input — a £20 cash short, a new supplier, a weird charge — it's one WhatsApp message, not an email thread.

✓

Month-end in 3 days.

Your monthly P&L is signed off within three working days of the month closing. Your VAT return is ready within the quarter deadline. Year-end pack goes to your accountant clean.

The whole thing takes one call.

Thirty minutes on a video call to connect your systems. First signed-off P&L within the month. Cancel any month. No setup fee.
