How it works

Three steps. Then it runs.

We connect to the systems you already use. Software does the heavy data work overnight. A real bookkeeper reviews it the next morning. You see the result before service.

  1. 01 Connect

    We plug into your till, bank and suppliers.

    One 30-minute setup call. We link your EPOS, your bank, and give your suppliers a forwarding email for invoices. Nothing changes about how you run service.

  2. 02 Run

    Overnight, every night, we do the books.

    Sales posted by 9am. Invoices coded. Bank reconciled to the penny. AI does the data work in minutes instead of days. If anything looks off, we hold the line and ask you one short question by message.

  3. 03 Review

    A bookkeeper reviews and signs off.

    Your assigned bookkeeper checks the work, signs the month, and is your direct contact. Same person, every month. Your year-end accountant still files the tax return — we just hand them clean books.
