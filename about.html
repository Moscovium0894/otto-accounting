About The Growthway

A back office
that runs itself.

The Growthway was built by a former chef-turned-engineer and a restaurant group CFO who were tired of bookkeeping that arrived three weeks after it mattered. We make live bookkeeping the way the back office actually wants to work — fast, accurate, and signed by a real person.

The story.

2024

An owner's complaint.

One of our founders was running a 3-site restaurant group and getting monthly P&Ls three weeks late. By the time the numbers landed, the food cost problem had cost him another £8,000.

2025

The first prototype.

We connected a single Toast EPOS to a Xero ledger and let an AI code the supplier invoices overnight. The next morning, the books matched. We knew it could work.

2026

The Growthway today.

86 independent restaurants and pubs onboarded across the UK, six qualified bookkeepers on the team, and a signoff SLA of three working days from month-end.
