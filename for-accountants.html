For accountants

Clean books. No shoebox.

Your restaurant clients should not be the worst part of your March. Otto runs their monthly bookkeeping to a standard you'd sign off on — which is the point — and hands you a tidy year-end pack instead of a carrier bag of receipts.

What you get in the year-end pack.

01

Reconciled to the penny.

Every bank line matched. Every till variance investigated. Every supplier statement reconciled at the end of the period. No surprises in the trial balance.

02

Coded the way you'd code it.

COGS split into food, wet, packaging. Wages split into BOH/FOH. Capex separated from R&M. The chart of accounts is yours — tell us once, we'll keep it that way.

03

Audit trail you can defend.

Each posted entry links to its source: the till's Z-read, the supplier PDF, the bank line. A junior can answer any HMRC query without you re-doing the year.

How we hand it over.

→

Your software, not ours.

We keep the books in Xero or QuickBooks — whichever your firm uses — so the year-end pack arrives in the tool you're already in. No export, no re-import.

One point of contact.

You speak to the bookkeeper who signs the file each month — by name, not a ticket queue. They know the year by the time you ask.
